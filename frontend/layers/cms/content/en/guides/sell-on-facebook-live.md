## Before you go live

### Connect your page

Open **Social → Channels** in the dashboard and connect your Facebook page.

### Give each product a code

Short codes like `A1`, `A2` are easiest for customers to type.

## During the live

Customers comment the code and quantity, for example `A1 2`. zaokaiy creates an order and sends them a checkout link in Messenger.

## After the live

Open **Social → Orders** to confirm payments and print shipping labels.
