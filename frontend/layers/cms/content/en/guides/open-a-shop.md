## What you need

- A phone number or email address
- A few product photos (at least 500 px on the short side)
- For a registered business: your company documents

## Steps

### 1. Create your account

Tap **Open a shop** at the top of the page and sign up with Google, Facebook, WhatsApp or email.

### 2. Choose your shop type

Pick what you sell — products, food, vehicles or insurance. You can't change this later, so choose carefully.

### 3. Add your first product

Open **Products → New product**, add photos, a name, a price and stock, then save.

::tip
Start typing the product name — zaokaiy suggests photos you can reuse.
::

### 4. Publish

New products are checked by our team before they go live. You'll see the status in the product list.
