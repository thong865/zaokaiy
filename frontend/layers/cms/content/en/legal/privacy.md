## What we collect

### Account details

Your name, email or phone number, and — for business shops — the company documents you upload for verification. Verification documents are stored encrypted and are never public.

### Orders

Delivery names, addresses and phone numbers, so shops and couriers can deliver your order.

## How we use it

- To run your account, shop and orders
- To prevent fraud, including cash-on-delivery refusals
- To improve zaokaiy (aggregated statistics only)

## Who we share it with

Only the shop you order from and the courier delivering it. We don't sell personal data.

## Your choices

You can update your details on the **Account** page, or ask us to delete your account.
