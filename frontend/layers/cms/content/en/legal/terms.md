## 1. About these terms

These terms apply when you use zaokaiy — the website, the seller dashboard and the POS app. By creating an account you agree to them.

## 2. Your account

### Who can sign up

You must be at least 18 years old, or have permission from a parent or guardian.

### Keeping it safe

- Keep your password and your recovery codes private.
- Turn on two-step verification for shops with staff.
- Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

## 3. Selling on zaokaiy

### What you may sell

Only products you have the right to sell, described honestly, with real photos and prices.

### Prohibited items

Weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, wildlife products and anything illegal in Laos.

### Orders and delivery

Ship orders on time and keep the order status up to date. Cash-on-delivery parcels that customers refuse may be reported.

## 4. Payments and fees

Fees are shown in the dashboard before you agree to them. We may change fees with 30 days' notice.

## 5. Ending your account

You can close your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.
